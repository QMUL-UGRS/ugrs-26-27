date: 2025-10-08

room: "Mathematical Sciences Building, MB-204 (or via Teams)"
abstract: >
  The Langlands program is one of the largest webs of conjectures in
  mathematics — it generalises the correspondence that Andrew Wiles proved
  in 1997 to solve the over 300-year-old problem of Fermat's Last Theorem,
  and has many far-reaching implications. This talk gives an explicit
  example of the kind of correspondence the Langlands program predicts.
