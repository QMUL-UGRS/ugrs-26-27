title: "Linear Algebra Methods for Hyperbolic Partial Differential Equations"
speaker: "Claudia Garetto"
date: 2026-02-11
time: "15:00–16:00"
room: "Mathematical Sciences Building, MB-203 (or via Teams)"
abstract: >
  This talk shows the linear algebra methods used to study hyperbolic
  partial differential equations. These equations arise naturally in
  physics, for instance in the propagation of waves in a multi-layered
  medium. Particular attention is given to the roots of the equation and
  their multiplicities; given some initial conditions, we prove that a
  solution exists and is unique.
