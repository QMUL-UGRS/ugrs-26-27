title: "Credit Risk as Classification: Applying Machine Learning in Default Estimation"
speaker: "Wayne Xiaocheng"
date: 2025-10-29
time: "15:00–16:00"
room: "Mathematical Sciences Building, MB-204 (or via Teams)"
abstract: >
  Banks often model credit risk as the probability of default (PD) — the
  likelihood a borrower will default on a loan. We can frame this as a binary
  classification problem: default (bad credit) vs. non-default (good
  credit). This talk applies a traditional logistic regression model
  alongside a neural network for PD estimation, using real-world credit
  data, and discusses model concepts, performance, and calibration of
  predicted probabilities, emphasising interpretability and practical
  considerations.
