abstract: >
  In tropical mathematics, instead of adding we take the minimum of two
  numbers (or the maximum, if you swing that way), and instead of
  multiplying we add. Far from being a silly diversion in abstract algebra,
  the techniques of tropical mathematics are especially useful in geometry —
  thus many mathematicians just say "tropical geometry" — with applications
  in optimisation, economics, phylogenetics, and the theory of neural
  networks. This talk introduces tropical mathematics, including the
  unfortunate reason for the name, and a few of the things you can do with
  it.
