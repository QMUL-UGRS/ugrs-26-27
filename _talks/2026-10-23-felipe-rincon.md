abstract: >
  Felipe Rincón studies what geometry looks like when you strip it down to
  combinatorics. His research connects matroid theory — an abstraction of the idea
  of independence that shows up in linear algebra, graphs and beyond — with
  tropical geometry, where addition becomes "take the minimum" and multiplication
  becomes addition, and curves and surfaces turn into piecewise-linear skeletons.
  He holds an EPSRC grant on matroids in tropical geometry, works on building
  algebraic foundations for the subject, and regularly brings the international
  matroid and tropical geometry community to Queen Mary for workshops.
