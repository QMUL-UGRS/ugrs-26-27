  Tarek Anous works on black holes and what happens inside them, accelerating

  geometry and more like a material to be probed in a lab, with the aim of
  understanding the big bang's imprint on the universe and the interiors of black
  holes. Before Queen Mary he spent nine years as a postdoc at MIT, UBC and
