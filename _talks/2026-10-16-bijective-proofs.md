abstract: >
  Sometimes the cleanest way to prove two quantities are equal isn't to compute
  either of them — it's to find a matching between the two sets they count. This
  talk works through a few classic bijective proofs, building up to an open
  problem in the field that's resisted a bijective argument for decades.
