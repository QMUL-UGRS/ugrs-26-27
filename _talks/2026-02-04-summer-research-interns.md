abstract: >
  Every year QMUL funds some students to do eight-week-long summer research
  internships. This talk presents an overview of the projects that some of
  the previous research interns worked on — implementation of algorithms for
  Monte Carlo simulation, and searches for solutions to hard combinatorial
  problems. One project supervised by the speaker looks deeper into the
  no-three-in-line problem, which asks how many sites of an n×n grid can be
  occupied such that no three of them are collinear, using methods as varied
  as AI, machine learning, and constraint programming.
