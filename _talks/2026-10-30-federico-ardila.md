abstract: >
  Federico Ardila works at the meeting point of combinatorics, algebra and
  geometry, on matroids, polytopes, tropical geometry and combinatorial Hodge
  theory — an area where he and his collaborators have built the basic objects
  everyone else now uses and settled conjectures that had stood for decades. He has
  been an invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians, a Clay
  Lecturer at the British Combinatorics Conference and a member of the Institute
  for Advanced Study, and joined Queen Mary in 2026 from San Francisco State
  University on a Royal Society Wolfson Fellowship. He is also known well beyond
  his research for his work on building a mathematical community that is welcoming
  to everyone, including the ECCO school in Colombia and his essay "Todos Cuentan".
