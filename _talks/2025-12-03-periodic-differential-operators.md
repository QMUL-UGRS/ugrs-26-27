title: "Periodic Differential Operators"
speaker: "Matthew Lewis"
date: 2025-12-03
time: "15:00–16:00"
room: "Mathematical Sciences Building, MB-204 (or via Teams)"
abstract: >
  The long-term effect of the repeated application of a matrix can be
  categorised by its eigenvalues. But differential equations are not
  matrices, so can they have eigenvalues? Dynamical systems with seasonal
  behaviour can be modelled with periodic differential operators, and this
  talk shows how eigenvalues can still be used to categorise the stability
  of their solutions.
