title: "Counting Small Subgraphs"
speaker: "Madhumitha Krishnakumar"
date: 2026-02-18
time: "15:00–16:00"
room: "Mathematical Sciences Building, MB-203 (or via Teams)"
abstract: >
  Graph motifs are small patterns that occur in graphs at a higher rate than
  chance would allow. Motif counting has a myriad of applications, including
  in gene regulatory networks, biological networks, and the evolution of
  cancerous tumours. This talk introduces graph motif counting problems and
  shows how counting small subgraphs can be reduced to counting graph
  homomorphisms.
tags: [graph-theory, combinatorics]
