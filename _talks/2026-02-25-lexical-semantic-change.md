title: "Rethinking Metrics for Lexical Semantic Change Detection"
speaker: "Roksana Goworek"
date: 2026-02-25
time: "15:00–16:00"
room: "Mathematical Sciences Building, MB-203 (or via Teams)"
abstract: >
  Words change meaning over time — the words "cloud" or "plane" mean
  something very different than they did 150 years ago. This talk looks at
  how we can measure these meaning changes across large historical and
  modern corpora, using AI language models that represent individual usages
  of words as vectors. Most existing methods rely on just a couple of
  standard distance measures to compare meanings across time; this talk
  introduces two new, intuitive measures that compare word usages across
  time periods more locally, showing through experiments on multiple
  languages and models that these new measures are often more reliable,
  especially when models are less specialised or data is reshaped into fewer
  vector dimensions.
