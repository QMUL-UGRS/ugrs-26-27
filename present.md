<h1>Present a talk</h1>
<p class="subhead">Speakers are QMUL researchers — academic staff, postdocs, and occasionally PhD students. Self-nominations are just as welcome as nominating someone else.</p>

## What to expect

- **Format:** 20 minutes of talk, 10 minutes of questions.
- **Audience:** undergraduates from across the university, mostly outside your specific field — the talk should be followable without specialist background in the topic.
- **Content:** your own research, at whatever stage it's at. A talk about an ongoing project or an open problem you're excited about works just as well as a talk about a finished result.
- **Slides:** optional, not required. Whiteboard-only talks are fine.
- **Recording:** talks are recorded by default so people who miss the session can catch up, unless you'd rather not — just let the organisers know.

## How to propose a speaker

Email the organisers with:

1. The speaker's name and department (or "me," if that's you)
2. A rough working title
3. A 2–3 sentence description of what the talk will cover
4. Which weeks work, if known

<p><a class="btn btn-primary" href="{{ site.propose_talk_url }}">Propose a speaker by email</a></p>

## Code of conduct

This seminar follows QMUL's standard expectations for respectful academic discussion. Disagreement with ideas is welcome; personal criticism isn't. If something happens at a session that you want to flag, contact the organisers directly.
