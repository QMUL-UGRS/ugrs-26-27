About

The {{ site.title }} is a weekly student-run seminar at {{ site.university }}'s {{ site.department }}, where mathematics researchers present their work directly to an audience of undergraduates. Speakers are invited by the organising committee. The goal is exposure to what mathematical research actually looks like, pitched at a level any undergraduate can follow — you don't need to be a maths student to come along. It meets in the {{ site.venue_address }}. See the official seminar page at QMUL for more.
